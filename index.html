Before you choose

A few good questions.

How much fabric will my sofa need?

Share the sofa dimensions and a few photographs with the showroom. Quantity depends on its shape, cushioning, pattern matching and the upholsterer’s cutting plan.

Can I see the actual colour and texture?

Ask the showroom about available physical swatches. Screen colours are inspiration; inspect the actual fabric under your room’s lighting before deciding.

How do I enquire or place an order request?

Choose a material or complete the contact form. Review your message, open WhatsApp and tap Send. The showroom will need to confirm price, stock and delivery before the order is final.
